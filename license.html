After successful payment, your files (WAV + Trackout) will be sent to your WhatsApp or Email within 24 hours. You will receive a download link (Google Drive / WeTransfer).

Since this is a digital product delivered instantly upon purchase, refunds are not offered once the files have been sent. Please listen to the full preview before purchasing.

You cannot resell or share the beat file itself (the WAV/trackout) with anyone else — for example, uploading it to another beat-selling site, or sending the raw instrumental to another artist.

You can freely release your finished song made using this beat — on Spotify, YouTube, Instagram, or anywhere else — and earn money from it. That's exactly what exclusive rights are for.
